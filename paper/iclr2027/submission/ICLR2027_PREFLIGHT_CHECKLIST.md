# ICLR 2027 Preflight Checklist

## Dates And Accounts

- [ ] Every author has an up-to-date OpenReview profile, ideally with an institutional email.
- [ ] Submit a genuine abstract by **2026-09-18 23:59 AOE**.
- [ ] Finalize all authors before the abstract deadline: authors cannot be added or removed afterward.
- [ ] Submit paper and supplementary material by **2026-09-25 AOE**.
- [ ] Check the ICLR author quota: no author on more than 20 submissions.
- [ ] Ensure reciprocal-reviewing obligations are met. Every submission needs at least one eligible author registered to review at least three papers, unless all authors qualify for the stated exemption.

## Double Blind

- [ ] Paper PDF has anonymous authors and affiliations; `\\iclrfinalcopy` remains commented out.
- [ ] Main text, appendix, supplement, PDF metadata, filenames, URLs, repository history, and video/demo hosting do not reveal author identities.
- [ ] Related work by the authors is cited in third person.
- [ ] Any code artifact is anonymous and does not expose commit identities, author names, emails, organization names, or visitor tracking.
- [ ] No acknowledgements, funding text, lab names, or institutional compute identifiers appear in the submission version.

## Format And Content

- [ ] Official 2027 style files are used without modification.
- [ ] Main text is no more than nine pages at initial submission; references are after the main text.
- [ ] Appendix follows references and contains all nonessential but reproducibility-critical detail.
- [ ] Core experimental evidence is in the main paper, not only in the appendix or supplement.
- [ ] Figures remain distinguishable in grayscale and captions state the experimental condition, aggregation, and uncertainty.
- [ ] All claims, numbers, and plots are traceable to saved configs and generated artifacts.

## Required And Recommended Statements

- [ ] Add the required AI-use disclosure in the paper and report matching information in the submission form.
- [ ] The disclosure covers required categories relevant here: conceptual/methodological feedback, experiment design, implementation, synthetic-data or toy-case generation if applicable, and interpretation of results. It states that authors verified the work and take responsibility.
- [ ] Include a reproducibility statement before references that points to configs, data processing, seeds, code, and appendix details.
- [ ] Include an ethics statement if appropriate; current ICLR guidance recommends one for work with relevant concerns and allows up to one exempt page.
- [ ] All authors read and acknowledge the ICLR Code of Ethics.

## Final OpenReview Pass

- [ ] Title/abstract match the actual paper; neither is a placeholder.
- [ ] PDF and all supplementary materials are uploaded before the paper deadline.
- [ ] Validate the rendered PDF locally: page count, anonymity, references, hyperlinks, figures, and font embedding.
- [ ] Save the exact submitted PDF, source snapshot, supplementary ZIP checksum, OpenReview submission ID, and deadline confirmation in this directory.
